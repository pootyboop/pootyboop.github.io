⌨ DEVELOPMENT

In 2023, a couple years prior joining Reflex Arc as an XR Developer, I interned to work on Lord Whitney's The Chime Hours, a mixed reality experience where participants move from the real world into a VR forest they can physically explore together. Planning and aligning the transition between the real-life warehouse and Unreal Engine forest was super cool. We prepared the transition so that participants put on the headsets when entering the space and saw a direct 1:1 camera feed of the real warehouse, but as they physically walked inside, the forest faded into view. Participants could see each other represented by avatars in the forest, and were free to explore the environment, reveal trees around them with their flashlights, and find a character that led them through the forest.
